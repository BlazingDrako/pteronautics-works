Pteronautics · Works · free and open source

Design the whole vehicle. Every analysis follows.

Works is a design program for whole aircraft that runs in your browser. Change the wing, and the aerodynamics, mass and balance, stability, performance and requirements recompute together, on the same validated solvers as Pteronautics Atlas.

No install and no account. Your designs stay in your browser, and save as plain .json files.

How it works

One edit. Every analysis follows.

Each analysis declares what it reads from the vehicle and which results it needs. An edit marks only what depends on it as stale, and the coupling graph reruns those, in order. Try it: these sliders drive the real graph.

Airfoil lab

Zoom from the vehicle into the boundary layer.

Pick any station on the wing. The lab runs mfoil, a viscous panel method with a coupled boundary layer and eN transition, at that station's chord, Reynolds number and local lift at cruise, off the main thread.

  • Pressure on both surfaces against the inviscid solution
  • The boundary layer drawn on the section: laminar, turbulent, separated
  • Polars swept in the background, and one click to put a better airfoil on the wing

Fidelity you choose

Swap a model. See what it changes.

Every node in the graph says which model it runs. Move the wing from lifting line to vortex lattice, or the sections from thin-airfoil theory to viscous polars, and everything downstream reruns with it.

Kestrel, thin airfoil against viscous sections

Running the viscous polars…

Computed on this page. Thin airfoil uses a handbook cℓ,max and fully turbulent skin friction (conservative); viscous uses mfoil polars with free transition on a clean wing (optimistic). The real aircraft sits between the two.

Requirements, live

The spec is part of the design.

Targets sit next to the numbers they constrain, with the margin and status recomputed on every edit. A margin under 5% is marginal, so you see trouble coming before it fails.
